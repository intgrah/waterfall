One engine.
Two ways to search.

Search backtracks over alternatives and their pending obligations. Committed follows an ACL2-inspired policy that accepts the first local progress.

Both construct proofs checked by Lean. Commitment prunes alternatives; it can be useful, but it is not a universal speedup.

Effort funds deeper plans and stronger individual operations as well as more attempts. Lean's enclosing heartbeat and recursion limits still apply. More effort does not guarantee a shorter running time. Multiple CPUs explore different trials of the same policy with shared total budgets; dedicated worker threads avoid contention with Lean’s elaboration task pool. Fixed-budget coverage can change.

Bring useful structure.

Waterfall is designed for recursive functions and inductive data, particularly when helper lemmas are available. Use it for an entire theorem or to close branches after your own setup.

Missing lemmas, difficult mutual induction and poor ordering remain real limitations. This is a Lean-native research tool inspired by ACL2, not a complete ACL2 implementation.

Keep control of search.

Lean functions configure candidate ordering, costs, depth and strength schedules, and traversal. Custom policies use the same metered transitions and compatible proof checkpoints.
