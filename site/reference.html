Tactic options

Both waterfall and waterfall? accept individual options or a structure such as (config := {mode := .search, effort := 3000}).

OptionDefaultBehavior
mode.searchBacktracking search or ACL2-inspired .committed.
cpus1Maximum concurrent workers, with a shared total budget.
effort1000Number of attempts across all trials, including failures.
attemptHeartbeats20000000Base raw heartbeat slice per operation; trial strength scales it.
lazytrueTrue generates a batch when search reaches it; false generates all batches in a phase up front.
deferChecksfalseTrue postpones applicability checks until a candidate is considered.
reportfalseTrue prints search statistics.

Hooks

Waterfall.run takes a Config, supplied rules and Hooks. The hooks contain the search policy and callbacks for ordering, costs and trial schedules. A mode’s existing hooks can be updated with ordinary Lean record syntax:

example (P : Prop) (h : P) : P := by
  run_tac discard <| Waterfall.run {} #[]
    { Waterfall.Mode.search.hooks with
      trials := Waterfall.diagonalTrials 1 }

trials chooses depth/strength pairs. order reorders candidates within a batch; the engine checks that none were added or dropped. cost assigns path costs subject to the engine’s minimums. extraMoves adds proof operations.

Policies and proof state

A Node saves the Lean proof state, all pending goals and the proof steps taken so far. SearchPolicy.choose receives lazily generated successor nodes and decides which to follow. Its state can hold a queue or another search frontier.

Space.expand tries steps on a chosen goal. Space.restart restores a complete checkpoint and charges the restart against the budget. Neither operation separates a goal from its siblings: their metavariable assignments must remain consistent.

Choices.first commits to the first successor. filter removes choices, and collect evaluates and stores them all. Collecting choices therefore spends the time and budget required to generate them.

The policy tests include FIFO and scored selection. A custom policy can starve some branches or discard them; the engine does not make arbitrary policies complete.

Workers

Waterfall.Parallel.run runs trials in isolated workers. Each worker has its own mutable observers and Lean state. Only a complete proof is adopted. The other workers are cancelled cooperatively and joined before returning.

The API notes describe the budget accounting and callback initialization.

Timing and replay

Waterfall.Observe is an optional import. capture records inclusive and exclusive timings, raw heartbeats and the accepted plan. Control can reduce per-operation limits or impose a cooperative deadline.

replay executes the recorded steps without searching for alternatives. It checks operation identifiers, goal positions and generated subgoals against the recording, and rejects an incomplete proof. Replay requires the same source, rules and hooks.
